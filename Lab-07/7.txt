MATRIX CHAIN MULTIPLICATION

Let DP[i][j] represent the minimum number of scalar multiplications required to multiply matrices Ai through Aj.

The recurrence is:

DP[i][j] = min(DP[i][k] + DP[k+1][j] + p[i-1]p[k]p[j])

where k ranges from i to j-1.

There are O(n^2) DP states.

For each state, O(n) possible split points are checked.

Therefore:

Time Complexity:
O(n^3)

Space Complexity:
O(n^2)

The second table used to store the optimal split points also requires O(n^2) space.
