INVERT THE COIN TRIANGLE

Let n be the number of coins in the bottom row.

Total number of coins = n(n+1)/2 = O(n^2).

For the exhaustive validation algorithm, possible inverted-triangle positions are checked and for each position the overlap with the original triangle is calculated.

Time Complexity:
O(n^4)

Space Complexity:
O(n^2)

After deriving the mathematical formula:

Minimum moves = floor(n(n+1)/6)

the answer can be calculated directly.

Optimized Time Complexity:
O(1)

Optimized Space Complexity:
O(1)
