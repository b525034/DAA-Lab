Let DP[e][f] represent the minimum number of droppings required using e eggs and f floors.

The recurrence is:

DP[e][f] = 1 + min(max(DP[e-1][k-1], DP[e][f-k]))

where k ranges from 1 to f.

There are E x F DP states.

For each state, up to F possible values of k are checked.

Time Complexity:
O(EF^2)

Space Complexity:
O(EF)

For E = 2 and F = 100, the minimum number of droppings is 14
