SECURITY SWITCHES

Each switch can have two states: ON or OFF.

Therefore, for n switches, there are:

2^n

possible configurations.

The validation algorithm uses BFS and may visit all 2^n configurations.

For every configuration, up to n switches are checked to determine the legal moves.

Time Complexity:
O(n * 2^n)

Space Complexity:
O(2^n)

After deriving the direct formula:

Minimum moves = floor(2^(n+1)/3)

the result can be calculated directly.

Optimized Time Complexity:
O(1)

Optimized Space Complexity:
O(1)
