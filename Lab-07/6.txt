6.THE BEST TIME TO BE ALIVE

For n scientists, there are 2n events:

Birth = +1
Death = -1

All events are sorted according to their year. If birth and death occur in the same year, death is processed before birth.

Sorting 2n events takes:

O(n log n)

After sorting, all events are scanned once, which takes:

O(n)

Therefore:

Time Complexity:
O(n log n)

Space Complexity:
O(n)

The space is required to store the 2n events.
